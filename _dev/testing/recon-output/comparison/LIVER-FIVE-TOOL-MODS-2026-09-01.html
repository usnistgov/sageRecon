01 Why Preview was missing

Not a decision — an oversight plus two convention traps. VariableMods appears nowhere in NOTES, JOURNAL, PLAN, or any script in testing/scripts/, so no locked decision excluded it.

Cause 1 — catalogued as configuration

The vendored README lists objs/FixedMods*.txt, objs/VariableMods*.txt under "mod settings". VariableMods.txt is only half settings: it also carries nmods, n16, prop and prop2 — Preview's measured counts. Filed as config, it was never read as output. This is the same failure that hid Preview's mass-accuracy numbers in result_summary.html until 2026-09-01.

Cause 2 — the alkylation-agnostic frame

The mod comparison is built on four tools that discovered +57 rather than assuming it. Preview had CysMod=+57.021464 pre-set by the operator, so it cannot join that claim. But that argument only excludes Preview from the +57-on-C row — it was applied to the whole arm.

The two conventions that must be undone first

Convention 1 — masses are relative to the fixed mod

Cysteine chemistry sits 57.021464 off the axis

Rows named (-fixed mod) are deltas relative to the fixed +57 on C. On the raw axis they match nothing. Verified three ways against Preview's own detail text, not assumed:

Trioxidation  -9.036720 + 57.021464 =  +47.984744   detail: "C[+48]"
Propionamide +14.015650 + 57.021464 =  +71.037114   detail: "C[+71]"
Dehydro      -58.029289 + 57.021464 =   -1.007825   detail: "unmodified_C[-2]"

3 x oxidation = 47.984745                       <- matches to 1e-6

Uncorrected, a mass join silently drops every cysteine modification Preview found.

Convention 2 — one total, many rows

Group totals repeat across site rows

Exactly the Mascot multi-site trap already in NOTES. Deamidated is listed at N and at Q, both showing 237 — because 237 is the combined total (194 N + 43 Q). Summing gives 474.

Worse, one group can span two masses: pyro-glu lists 66 against both −17.0265 and −18.0106, where the detail page gives the true split as 61 and 5. Deduped on (mod, mass, count, denominator); 9 repeated rows dropped, 4 group totals replaced by the detail split.

Left unsplit

One group the detail page never splits: "Carbamidomethylation artifacts (H,K,N-terminus[+57/+114]): 2.3% (54/2359)". Its 54 is repeated on +57.0215 and +114.0429, marked ᵍ below. Do not read those two cells as independent.

The result

recon's top four recommendations are found by every other tool. Oxidation, Carbamidomethyl, Deamidation and Gln->pyro-Glu are 4/4. Two more are 3/4. Nothing recon recommends is invented: every one of the nine is found by at least one independent tool, and the two that only Preview misses are ones Preview had no candidate for — its variable list is a fixed menu of 44 entries, not an open window.

+57 is 3 discoveries and 1 assumption

Preview's 2.34 % at +57.0215 is not cysteine carbamidomethylation. It is the H/K/N-term artifact. Preview's cysteine +57 was pre-set by the operator and reported separately as a fixed mod at 100.0 % (226/226) — a confirmation of the input, not a detection. So the row is corroborated by Shepherd, Mascot and MetaMorpheus, with Preview counted apart.

The one to look at

−89.0302 Met-loss+Acetylation is recon's weakest recommendation: Mascot alone has it, at 0.17 %. recon's internal evidence is strong — odds ratio 8578, q = 6.5e−152, 72 PSMs, protein N-terminal with Met loss — and neither Shepherd nor MetaMorpheus reports the mass at all. That is a genuine single-source recommendation, not a consensus one, and should be labelled as such wherever the nine are quoted.

03 The proline oxidation recon does not report

Preview reports "Hydroxyproline: 1.5% (18/1166) of peptides containing P" as a line of its own. recon has no such recommendation. Three questions follow, and all three have answers on disk.

1. Is it in recon's curated list? Yes.

Not a curation gap. reference-notes/metaMorpheusMods/Mods.txt carries the entry, and that file is compiled into the binary:

ID   Hydroxylation
TG   P
PP   Anywhere.
MT   Common Biological
CF   O1                       -> 15.994915 Da
Which files recon actually loads

From the build dependency graph, four of the eight files in that directory are compiled in: Mods.txt, aListOfmods.txt, ProteaseMods.txt, surfactants.txt. ptmlist.txt, glyco.txt, substitutions.txt and tmt.txt are not. Hydroxyproline appears in both ptmlist.txt (unloaded) and, as Hydroxylation on P, in Mods.txt (loaded) — so it is available to the classifier.

3. Why recon misses it: one mass, one recommendation

recon's open search assigns a delta per PSM, not a site. Everything at +15.9945 is one peak of 1,688 PSMs. Four curated entries sit within CURATED_TOL_DA = 0.010 Da of it, including Hydroxylation on P — the gap is 0.00044 Da, well inside the window — and each is tested for residue enrichment with a BH-corrected q. Then:

// STATISTICS PATH — take the best supported candidate.
let best = testable
    .iter()
    .filter(|(_, _, r)| { let (o, q) = r.unwrap(); o >= OR_MIN && q < Q_MAX })
    .max_by(...odds ratio...);          // tier_assignment.rs:404
The mechanism — measured, not inferred

peptide_hits asks "does this peptide contain an acceptor residue". It is not a localization. So each candidate is scored against the background rate at which peptides contain that residue at all. Reproducing the test on the run's own PSMs:

Correction to an earlier reading

Hydroxylation on P does not lose a max_by tie-break — it fails the OR ≥ 2 gate outright, at ~1.34. Proline sits in 56.0 % of all liver peptides, so a containment test cannot see enrichment for it, and the band is 92 % M-oxidised peptides that merely also happen to contain a proline. It would not be recommended even if max_by kept every passing candidate rather than one.

What this is and isn't

This is not a missing curated entry, and not a missed peak — recon found the mass, ranked it second overall, and recommended it. It is a reporting-shape limit: the recommendation is keyed to a mass, and a mass can carry more than one real modification. Collagen and ECM proteins in liver make proline oxidation the case where that bites hardest, which is why all four reference tools list it separately.

04 How general is this?

Proline is not a special case. Across the four curated files actually compiled into the binary, 58.6 % of entries sit at a mass shared with at least one other entry — so the one-recommendation-per-mass rule has to pick a winner far more often than not.

Census

99 entries, 62 masses

21 mass clusters hold more than one candidate at 0.010 Da. 58 entries (58.6 %) live in one. The largest cluster holds 5. Of those, 15 masses have two or more residue-testable candidates competing.

The bias

The rarest acceptor wins

To clear OR ≥ 2 against a background containment f, a candidate needs band containment x = 2f/(1+f). A rare acceptor needs little; a common one needs nearly everything. At +57.0215, Carbamidomethyl on C needs 20.6 % while the broad Carbamidomethyl DEHKSTY needs 99.7 %.

Difficulty, not prediction

The needed-fraction is what a candidate would have to reach, not what it will reach — that depends on the actual band. Both outcomes occur on this one file: +15.9949 Hydroxylation on P needs 71.8 %, has 62.7 %, and fails; −17.0265 Gln->pyro-Glu on Q needs 63.3 %, has a band that is nearly all Q, scores OR 650 and is recon's recommendation. The test works when one modification dominates its mass band. What it cannot do is surface a second, co-occurring modification at the same mass.

Not a verdict on the design

None of this says Hydroxylation on P should be recommended — its measured odds ratio is 1.34, and the gate is doing what it was built to do. Whether a mass should be allowed more than one recommendation, and whether a containment test is the right instrument for a common acceptor, are design questions this measurement opens rather than settles.

07 Reading rules

  • The columns are five different currencies. recon and Shepherd report % of PSMs; MetaMorpheus % of q ≤ 0.01 peptides; Mascot its own resolved-ET fraction; Preview nmods over a 2667-ID baseline, which is the number its summary page prints. NOTES "Prevalence currency" governs. Compare presence and rank, never magnitude.
  • Preview's list is closed. Its 19 masses come from a fixed menu of 44 candidate rows, not an open window. A Preview blank means "not on the menu", which is not the same as "searched and not found" — unlike a Shepherd or recon blank.
  • Mascot's 351 rows are site rows rolled up by mass, using the shipped adapter verbatim; 16 rows had no clean Unimod mass and were skipped and counted, never dropped silently.
  • Not equal populations. Preview kept ProtScore ≥ 20 representative proteins; recon Pass 1 is the whole database; the FragPipe and MetaMorpheus runs are their own. Same file, same FASTA, different peptide sets.
  • The MetaMorpheus arm needs a gitignored input (Task3-SearchTask/AllPeptides.psmtsv). The producer hard-stops with a named missing input rather than quietly dropping the arm.
